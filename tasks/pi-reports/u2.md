## Summary

Audited ModelsScreen, TransformsScreen, StylesScreen, PersonalizationScreen and
DictationPolishSections (plus reserved PolishPlayground, report only). Fixed 14
in-ownership defects (dead Dismiss control, stale keychain host, five silent

7 new).
